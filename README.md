# DIY Quarto

DIY Quarto is an interactive tool for learning how early modern
printers assembled a book. Working from digital images of the
Folger Shakespeare Library's copy of the second quarto of
*Hamlet* (1604, STC 22276), it walks through how individual
pages were arranged non-sequentially on a full sheet of paper,
then folded and cut, to produce the finished pamphlet -- a
process that's invisible once a book is bound.

## Origins

DIY Quarto grew out of conversations among college faculty and
Folger staff during the Folger Institute's 2016-17 micro-grant
initiative *Teaching Shakespeare to Undergraduates*, funded by
the National Endowment for the Humanities. It was developed as
part of the Folger's commemoration of the 400th anniversary of
Shakespeare's death.

- Editors: Kathleen Lynch and Justine DeCamillis
- Designer/Developer: Rebecca Niles (Virtual Printing House)
- Digital Managing Editor: Esther French

The Folger's own presentation of the project, including
downloadable PDF facsimiles, is archived at:
https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeare-in-print/diy-quarto/

This repository has been released publicly as free/open-source
software; it is no longer an actively maintained Folger product.
